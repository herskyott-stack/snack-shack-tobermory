Why people stop

Rated 4.6 stars on Google across hundreds of reviews, Snack Shack is known as Tobermory's halal stop — reviewers single out the warm welcome from owner Dean and his team, the generous portions and the quick service after a day on the water. Outdoor seating under the arbor, free parking, and dogs are welcome.
